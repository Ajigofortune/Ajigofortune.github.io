Who Owes Me (debt tracker app)

What I built

An installable app for small businesses to record who owes them money. It shows what is overdue or due soon, sends a polite WhatsApp reminder in one tap, records part-payments, adds due dates to the phone calendar, and has a PIN lock. Records stay on the user's phone and it works offline.

What it shows

Building a real tool for a real Nigerian problem with JavaScript, saving data on the device, and making a website installable like an app. I built it with AI tools while learning how the code works.
